## SVG style brief

- Solid monocolour silhouettes with no outline strokes.
- Smooth organic Bézier curves and strongly simplified anatomy.
- Large, rounded body masses with species identified primarily through silhouette and pose.
- Almost no facial detail; negative space is used sparingly, most visibly in the elephant ear and leg gaps.
- Calm, friendly, icon-like character energy rather than expressive cartoon faces.
- Best thumbnail treatment: preserve the bold silhouettes, recolour them with a small pastel palette, and add generous whitespace with high-contrast geometric type.

## Reference mode

Direct asset composition. The selected CC0 SVG silhouettes are recoloured and placed into the four-card layouts without redrawing their shapes. No image-generation model receives or recreates the source artwork.
